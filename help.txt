*COMMANDS*
Commands are listed as (name - alias: description)

The following commands do not initiate world tick:
	quit - q: force quits the game
	help - h: opens this help page
	left - l: rotates the player 90 degrees to the left
	right -r: rotates the player 90 degrees to the right

The following commands initiate world tick:
	move - m: moves the player on tile in current direction

Camera controls (do not initiate world tick):
	cw: move camera north (up)
	cs: move camera south (down)
	ca: move camera west (left)
	cd: move camera east (right)
	cc: center camera on player
